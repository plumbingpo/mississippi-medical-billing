Acclaim Radiology Management

Acclaim Radiology Management specializes in providing comprehensive revenue cycle management services specifically for radiology practices. They focus on maximizing reimbursement and streamlining billing processes for imaging centers and radiology groups.

Specialties Served: Radiology, Imaging Centers, Interventional Radiology, Teleradiology

Pricing Model: Percentage of collections (estimated 4-7%)

  • ✅ Deep expertise in the complexities of radiology coding and billing, leading to higher reimbursement rates.
  • ✅ Dedicated account managers with experience in radiology specific challenges.
  • ⚠️ Only serves radiology practices, not suitable for multi-specialty clinics.
3

Medical Management Solutions (MMS)

MMS offers a range of medical billing and practice management solutions designed to improve financial performance and operational efficiency for healthcare providers. They emphasize personalized service and advanced technology to reduce denials and accelerate cash flow.

Specialties Served: Primary Care, Internal Medicine, Cardiology, Orthopedics, Gastroenterology, Urology, Dermatology, Mental Health, Physical Therapy

Pricing Model: Percentage of collections (estimated 5-8%)

  • ✅ Broad expertise across numerous medical specialties, making them adaptable to diverse practices.
  • ✅ Focus on leveraging technology to automate processes and provide detailed reporting.
  • ⚠️ Larger client base might lead to less individualized attention compared to smaller, boutique firms.
4

AdvantEdge Healthcare Solutions

AdvantEdge provides end-to-end revenue cycle management services, including medical billing, coding, and practice management consulting. They aim to optimize financial performance and compliance for various healthcare organizations through a combination of technology and expert staff.

Specialties Served: Anesthesia, Pathology, Radiology, Cardiology, Emergency Medicine, Orthopedics, Ambulatory Surgery Centers

Pricing Model: Percentage of collections (estimated 4-7%)

  • ✅ Strong focus on analytics and reporting to identify areas for revenue improvement.
  • ✅ Expertise in complex specialties with high coding demands like Anesthesia and Pathology.
  • ⚠️ Their comprehensive service might be more expensive than basic billing-only providers.

Precision Practice Management

Precision Practice Management offers comprehensive medical billing and practice management services, utilizing advanced software and experienced billers to maximize collections and reduce administrative burden. They prioritize transparency and client communication.

Specialties Served: Family Practice, Internal Medicine, Pediatrics, OB/GYN, Ophthalmology, Chiropractic, Podiatry, Urgent Care

Pricing Model: Percentage of collections (estimated 4-8%)

  • ✅ Emphasis on personalized service and direct communication with dedicated billing specialists.
  • ✅ Strong track record in improving collection rates for a variety of common specialties.
  • ⚠️ May not have the same depth of expertise in highly niche or complex specialties as some larger firms.

Coronis Health

Coronis Health is a large, national revenue cycle management company that offers specialized billing solutions tailored to various healthcare sectors. They leverage advanced technology and a team of experts to optimize billing, coding, and collections for their clients.

Specialties Served: Anesthesia, Laboratory, Pathology, Radiology, Hospital, FQHC, Emergency Medicine, Urgent Care, Behavioral Health, Vision, Dental

Pricing Model: Percentage of collections (estimated 3.5-7%)

  • ✅ Extensive resources and technology due to their large scale, including sophisticated AI-driven tools.
  • ✅ Specialized teams for a wide array of healthcare sectors, ensuring deep industry knowledge.
  • ⚠️ Being a large national company, some practices might prefer a more local or boutique billing service.

MedMan (Medical Management & Billing)

MedMan provides medical billing and practice management services with a focus on improving practice efficiency and financial health. They offer a hands-on approach, working closely with practices to identify and address revenue cycle challenges.

Specialties Served: Primary Care, Pediatrics, Cardiology, Orthopedics, General Surgery, ENT, Urology, Pain Management

Pricing Model: Percentage of collections (estimated 5-9%)

  • ✅ Strong emphasis on proactive communication and partnership with the practice staff.
  • ✅ Customized reporting and analytics to provide actionable insights into financial performance.
  • ⚠️ Their pricing might be on the higher end for smaller practices compared to some more basic billing services.
